Setup

Getting Started

Adding stry to a server takes less than a minute.

1

Invite the bot

Open the invite link provided by the stry team, choose your server, and confirm. You need the Manage Server permission in that server to add a bot.

2

Review the requested permissions

Discord shows the permissions stry requests during the invite. Review them before confirming — a bot can only act through the permissions it is granted.

3

Check the role position

If you plan to use stry for moderation, place its role above the roles it needs to manage under Server Settings → Roles. Discord prevents bots from acting on members with equal or higher roles.

Removing the bot

You can remove stry at any time under Server Settings → Members. Removing the bot ends its access to your server; the handling of stored data is described in the Privacy Policy.

Built on automatic backups

Restoration never depends on the attacker's cooperation. Stry captures raid-safe snapshots of your server's channels and roles on a rolling schedule, so a compromised account cannot destroy the server faster than Stry can restore it. Every response is recorded as a numbered case your staff can review later.

Questions about panic mode or whitelist behavior? Ask in the support server.

Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the most common questions.

Who can add stry to a server?

Any member with the Manage Server permission. Bots cannot be added by regular members.

stry isn't responding in my server. What should I check?

First, confirm the bot is still a member of the server and is not offline. Then check that it has permission to view and send messages in the channel you are using, and that its role sits above the roles it needs to manage. If the problem persists, ask in the support server.

How do I report a bug or a security issue?

Report it in the support server. Good-faith security reports are always welcome — reporting a vulnerability responsibly is not treated as abuse.

Can loss of access be appealed?

No. Under the Terms of Service, removal of access for manipulation or abuse of the bot is permanent.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Data stored

The Bot stores the minimum data required to operate: server and user identifiers needed to remember configuration and settings, and records of actions performed through the Bot.

Data not collected

  • The content of your server's regular messages is not read or stored

Data is retained only while the Bot remains in your server. Removing the Bot leads to deletion of its stored configuration. To request deletion of specific records, contact the team in the support server.
